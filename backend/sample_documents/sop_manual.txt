MEDHA Standard Operating Procedure — Boiler Operation

Document ID: SOP-BOILER-003
Version: 2.1
Classification: internal

1. Purpose
   This SOP defines the safe operating procedures for the high-pressure
   boiler system at the MEDHA demonstration facility.

2. Scope
   Applies to all operators and engineers responsible for boiler start-up,
   normal operation, and shutdown.

3. Pre-startup Checklist
   - Verify water level in the drum is above the minimum mark.
   - Confirm all safety valves are tested and functional.
   - Check fuel gas pressure is within the operating range (2.5–3.0 bar).
   - Ensure the combustion air damper is in the correct position.
   - Verify the flue gas O₂ analyser reads 3–5%.

4. Normal Operation
   - Maintain steam pressure between 10.0 and 10.5 bar.
   - Monitor drum water level continuously.
   - Log all parameters every 30 minutes.

5. Emergency Shutdown
   - Trip the boiler if steam pressure exceeds 11.0 bar.
   - Trip the boiler if drum water level drops below the low-level alarm.
   - Close the main steam stop valve immediately upon trip.
   - Notify the shift supervisor and engineering team.
