MEDHA Asset Hierarchy and Tagging Policy

Document ID: POLICY-ASSET-001
Version: 1.0
Classification: public

Overview:
All physical assets within the MEDHA facility are assigned a unique tag
following the KKS (Kraftwerk-Kennzeichensystem) identification system.
This document defines the tagging conventions and hierarchy levels.

Hierarchy Levels:
1. System (Level 1): Major process systems — e.g., 10 = Boiler System
2. Equipment (Level 2): Individual equipment — e.g., 101 = Boiler drum
3. Component (Level 3): Sub-components — e.g., 101A = Level transmitter

Tagging Rules:
- Each asset receives a tag at Level 2 minimum.
- Tags are alphanumeric and case-insensitive.
- Deleted or decommissioned assets retain their tags as historical records.
- New tags must be registered with the Asset Management team before use.

Examples:
- P-101: Pump, tag number 101 in system P (Process)
- HE-201: Heat exchanger, tag number 201 in system HE (Heat Exchange)
- V-301: Vessel, tag number 301 in system V (Vessel)
