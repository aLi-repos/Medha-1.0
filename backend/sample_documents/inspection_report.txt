MEDHA Safety Inspection Report — Pump Unit P-101

Date: 2026-09-15
Inspector: Dr. Ananya Mehta
Classification: confidential

Summary:
Visual inspection of pump P-101 was conducted as part of the scheduled
preventive maintenance programme. The pump casing shows no visible
corrosion or mechanical damage. The bearing housing temperature was
recorded at 42°C, well within the acceptable range of 35–65°C.

Findings:
1. Slight oil seepage observed at the mechanical seal — recommend
   replacement of seal within the next 400 operating hours.
2. Motor vibration readings: X-axis 0.8 mm/s, Y-axis 0.7 mm/s,
   Z-axis 0.6 mm/s — all within acceptable limits (< 4.5 mm/s).
3. Impeller clearance measured at 0.35 mm — within specification
   of 0.3–0.4 mm.

Recommendation:
Continue monitoring. Schedule seal replacement at the next planned
shutdown. No immediate safety concerns identified.
